# Teiko Blocker privacy policy

_Last updated: 7 October 2026_

Teiko Blocker is a free browser extension that adds a pause before websites you choose. This policy explains what it does with information. In short: everything stays in your browser, and nothing is collected or sent anywhere.

## What Teiko Blocker stores

On your device, in your browser's extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`), Teiko Blocker saves:

- the websites on your block list, and the rules, schedules and time allowances you set for them;
- your settings, such as theme, focus timer routines and whether page notes are shown;
- a history of up to 500 recent blocked visits (the website and the time), used for the Insights page;
- time spent today on sites that have a daily allowance;
- if you set one, a salted, scrambled check (PBKDF2 hash) of your password. The password itself is never stored.

## What Teiko Blocker does not do

- It does not collect, sell or share any personal information.
- It does not send any information to the developer or to anyone else. It has no server, analytics, ads or trackers.
- It does not read the content of web pages.
- It does not load anything from the internet. Sites on your list are shown with a letter badge, not an icon fetched from the site.

## Website access

If you allow it, Teiko Blocker can act on websites so it can block the sites on your list. It only changes visits to those sites. It reads the address of the current tab when you open its toolbar button, and of the active tab when a site has a daily time allowance, to count that time. These addresses are not saved, except for the website names on your list and in your history.

You can take this access back at any time in your browser's extension settings.

## Your control

- **Export** saves your settings to a file you keep. The file includes your block list, settings, history and the password check, so keep it private.
- **Import** replaces your settings with a file you choose.
- **Clear data** in Settings deletes everything Teiko Blocker has stored. Removing the extension also deletes it.

## Children

Teiko Blocker does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
